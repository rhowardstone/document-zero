What this is

A daily record of what materially changed in American government and public life — and of what was reported without changing.

Items publish only when they are not already in general circulation.

Corrections

Editions are dated, permanent and never edited. A correction publishes as a new entry that supersedes the old, and both stay readable.

Sourcing

Every claim carries its source and a confidence. Where a primary document exists it is cited directly; where only secondary reporting exists, that is said.
