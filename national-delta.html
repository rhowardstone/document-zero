The map of every federal rule for the rest of the term published today, and essentially no one covered it

The 2026 Regulatory Plan and Unified Agenda hit the Federal Register this morning: more than 3,300 planned rulemaking actions across 78 agency agendas — reportedly the highest total in a decade. Under the administration's regulatory-budget framework, agencies have booked roughly $1.1 trillion in claimed net savings so far in FY2026, and this Agenda projects that to $1.5 trillion by the end of September.

This is the single document that tells you what the executive branch intends to do to every regulated industry between now and 2029. The Kennedy Center renamed itself a second time, against a standing court ruling

After a roughly two-hour Zoom meeting on August 13, the board voted 20–3 to rename the building "The John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts Restored and Renovated by President Donald J. Trump," rename the plaza "President Donald J. Trump Plaza," and close for two years of renovations.

The state change is not the vote. It is that this is the second attempt: in May, U.S. District Judge Christopher Cooper ruled that renaming the center after both Kennedy and Trump violated the federal statute that created the organization. The board acted anyway. Status is therefore ordered but not legally effective, with an obvious next filing.

Reading the vote for structure rather than outcome: the three votes against were Rep. Joyce Beatty, Sen. Sheldon Whitehouse, and Rep. Rick Larsen — the ex-officio congressional members. The only dissent came from the seats the board cannot appoint. Documented CBS · CNN

Said vs. did

the communications channel and the action channel, tracked separately — worked example: Iran

What was said

  • JUN 17 Trump and Pezeshkian sign an MOU declaring "removal of the naval blockade" and safe passage for commercial vessels at no charge — "for 60 days only."
  • AUG 14 Hegseth: the U.S. military has the capability to maintain its naval presence and enforce the blockade indefinitely.

What was done / observed

  • APR 7 Ceasefire halts major combat operations.
  • AFTER Iran routes vessels through its territorial waters rather than established lanes; attacks noncompliant ships. U.S. imposes blockade on Iranian ports.
  • EARLY AUG U.S. reimposes the blockade after renewed attacks on commercial vessels and several Arab states.
  • AUG 13–14 Two more ships attacked. Transit near standstill. World drawing down oil stockpiles.

The divergence is the story: an announced removal that never became an observed removal for more than a window, and a 60-day term that expires Saturday on an arrangement already abandoned.

Method, limits, and one thing this exercise settled

How this was made. One session, roughly twenty-five public web sources, no primary docket access, no wire feed, no database. Assembled by hand in the shape a pipeline would produce so the format can be judged before the pipeline is built.

What could not be reached. federalregister.gov redirected to an access interstitial, so the Unified Agenda's figures are second-hand and capped in confidence. BLS releases were not pulled. Polling was not pulled. The D.C. police order's date could not be resolved and is flagged rather than reported.

The thing worth arguing about. Applying your own provenance ceilings honestly, almost every claim on this page is source_type: news and therefore capped at 0.6. The only entries above that cap are the ones citing through to a primary document — a court allocution, a board vote, a congressional letter, a Federal Register publication. A national desk sourced from wire copy has a hard confidence ceiling of 0.6 and no way to raise it, because the primary documents are not in hand.

That is the honest argument for pointing this machinery at a corpus you actually hold. Three million documents on one server is the difference between a ledger that can say "the filing states" and one that can only ever say "outlets report." The national delta is a good demonstration. It is not, on this evidence, the strongest product.
